How it works

A Cutting Mechanism Like No Other.

One press, four things happen. All stainless steel. No electronics.
1 · Step

Foot presses the cap

The cap and plate move down together.

2 · Convert

Cam turns press into rotation

A helical slot on the cam shaft spins the rotor as the plate drives down.

3 · Shear

Blades cut hair at the inlet

The rotor blade sweeps past the fixed blades, shearing strands into short pieces that flush.

4 · Reset

Spring returns the cap

The cap rises back to flush. The rebound tells the user it worked.

In action

Press. Cut. Reset.

The blades cut by shearing, like scissors, so they never need to be sharp.
